About me

I built this site as a place to bring my work together in one spot and to practice the fundamentals of web development from the ground up. Rather than relying on a template or a page builder, I wanted to understand exactly how HTML and CSS fit together, down to details like linking a stylesheet correctly and choosing a color palette on purpose instead of by accident.

What motivated me most was wanting a home base that actually represents me, not a generic profile on someone else's platform. A personal site is something I control end to end: the layout, the colors, the words. Every choice on this page, from the fonts to the accent color, was made deliberately as part of learning how design decisions translate into code.

This page will keep growing as I add more projects and refine the design. For now, it's a starting point and a record of where I began.
